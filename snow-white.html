Snow White & the Seven Million Followers — a new musical

AMT Theater, New York · October 15–18, 2026

A darkly funny new musical about social media and AI, the collapse of authenticity, and the unsettling possibility that the most dangerous lies are the ones we most desperately want to believe.

Synopsis

In a not-so-distant future, AI has decimated the creator economy and left the world’s influencers fighting for their survival. A mysterious new social media platform offers them one last chance to maintain their fame, relevance, and livelihoods. But is the deal too good to be true?
